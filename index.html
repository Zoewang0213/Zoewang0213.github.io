Ziyi ‘Zoe’ Wang 王子一

Hi! I’m Ziyi 👋 I’m a Ph.D. student in Computer Science & Engineering at Texas A&M University, advised by Prof. Meng Xia.

My research uses human-centered methods to design, develop, and evaluate interactive systems that empower people to effectively leverage, adapt, and extend AI in their work and daily lives — to enhance their capabilities and augment their cognition.

Previously, I was a Master’s student in HCI at the University of Maryland, working with Dr. Zijian Ding and Prof. Fumeng Yang. I also collaborated with Prof. Yue Zhao, Prof. Xiyang Hu, and Prof. Xiang Yan. Before research, I worked as a designer on global brand design at NIO, HMI design at BMW, UX at Publicis Sapient and AI product management at bilibili — see my design work.
